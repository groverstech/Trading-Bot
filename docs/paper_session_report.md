# Paper-trading execution evidence (Phase 2)

All records below were generated by the running system and exported from its databases by `python -m grokbot.paper_report`. Each part ends with the independent audit (`grokbot.audit`), which recomputes every figure from fills and the ledger.

* **Part A: strategy forward-test.** The terminal ran unattended on genuine Binance data:
  * Spot from production REST + WebSocket.
  * Futures from the Binance Futures **testnet** feed, because production futures is geo-blocked in this environment.
  * Paper research mode was on, which let the (unpromoted) strategies trade on paper only.
  * Default risk limits, unchanged.
  * Entries were market orders first, then post-only limit orders.
  * The server was restarted three times with open positions to exercise recovery.
* **Part B: operator validation trades.** These are clearly-labelled trades (`strategy = validation`, not strategy signals) run through the unchanged pipeline (`python -m grokbot.paper_validation`). Their stop and target distances were close enough to trigger within the session, so that stop-loss and take-profit exits could be observed on real prices.

## Finding from the session (fixed)

The LINK short in Part A lost 0.768 USDT against 0.40 USDT of planned risk.

**Root cause:** after a restart, the first scan priced signals from the close of a candle that had closed 11–41 minutes earlier. The executable price had moved 0.7–1.6%, which doubled the real stop distance. The loss stayed inside the 1% max-risk cap, but the system now prevents it in three places:
1. The Scanner discards stale candles.
2. The Sizer sizes from the executable bid/ask.
3. The risk check `entry_drift` rejects adverse drift greater than 25% of the stop distance.

Regression tests replay the case. Details: `AUDIT.md` F11.

Trades opened after the fix (XRP, and all of Part B) show entry drift of at most 0.1%.
